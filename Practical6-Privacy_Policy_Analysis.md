# Practical 6: Privacy Policy Analysis

## Aim

To analyze the privacy policy of a company and identify privacy practices, gaps and areas for improvement.

## Company Selected

**Company:** Google

**Website:** https://www.google.com/

**Privacy Policy:** https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Requirements

- Internet connection
- Web browser
- Basic knowledge of data privacy

## Procedure

1. Visit Google's official Privacy Policy.
2. Identify the types of information collected.
3. Study the purposes for which the information is used.
4. Check how users can control or delete their information.
5. Examine data retention and sharing practices.
6. Identify gaps and suggest improvements.

## Observations

| Privacy Aspect | Observation | Area for Improvement |
|---|---|---|
| Data Collection | Google collects information such as account details, device information, activity and location depending on the services and settings used. | Explain data collection in a simpler way for users. |
| Purpose of Collection | Data is used to provide and improve services and personalize experiences. | Give clearer explanations for each major purpose. |
| User Control | Users have privacy settings and tools such as Privacy Checkup and My Activity. | Make privacy controls easier to find and understand. |
| Data Retention | Different types of data may be retained for different periods. | Provide clearer retention information for each major data category. |
| Data Deletion | Users can delete certain information and may delete their Google Account. | Clearly explain the complete deletion process and possible delays. |
| Third-Party Sharing | Information may be shared with third parties in specified circumstances. | Provide simple explanations of important sharing situations. |

## Privacy Gaps Identified

1. The privacy policy contains a large amount of information and may be difficult for ordinary users to understand.
2. Different services may have different data practices, making comparison difficult.
3. Retention periods vary depending on the type of data and settings.
4. Users may need to navigate several privacy controls to manage their information.

## Recommendations

1. Use simpler language and shorter explanations.
2. Provide a clear summary of important privacy practices.
3. Make retention periods easier to understand.
4. Provide a single, easily accessible privacy dashboard.
5. Clearly explain when information is shared with third parties.

## Result

The privacy policy of Google was analyzed and various data collection, user control, retention and sharing practices were identified. Areas for improvement were also suggested.

## Conclusion

Google provides privacy controls that allow users to review, manage and delete certain information. However, the large amount of information in the privacy policy can make it difficult for users to quickly understand how their data is handled. Simpler explanations and clearer information about retention and data sharing could improve transparency.
