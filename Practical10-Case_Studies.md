# Practical 10: Case Study of a Privacy Breach

## Aim

To study a real-world privacy breach, understand its causes and identify important lessons for protecting personal data.

## Case Study Selected

**Case:** Facebook–Cambridge Analytica Data Scandal

**Organizations Involved:** Facebook and Cambridge Analytica

## Introduction

The Facebook–Cambridge Analytica case involved the collection and use of personal information from Facebook users for purposes related to political advertising and profiling. The case raised major concerns about user consent, transparency and the use of personal data.

## What Happened?

A third-party application collected information from Facebook users and, at the time, also obtained information relating to some of their Facebook friends. The data was later used by Cambridge Analytica for purposes beyond what many affected users understood or expected.

## Privacy Issues Identified

1. Lack of clear understanding and transparency regarding data use.
2. Collection of data beyond the expectations of some users.
3. Third-party access to personal information.
4. Concerns about informed consent.
5. Improper use of personal data for profiling and targeting.

## Key Lessons

| Issue | Lesson |
|---|---|
| User Consent | Users should understand what they are agreeing to. |
| Data Collection | Organizations should limit collection to necessary data. |
| Third-Party Access | Third-party applications should be carefully controlled. |
| Transparency | Data practices should be clearly explained. |
| Accountability | Organizations should take responsibility for protecting user data. |

## Privacy Protection Strategies

1. Provide clear privacy notices.
2. Limit third-party access to personal data.
3. Collect only necessary information.
4. Regularly review applications and data-sharing practices.
5. Give users meaningful privacy controls.

## Result

The Facebook–Cambridge Analytica case was analyzed and key privacy risks and lessons related to consent, transparency, data collection and third-party access were identified.

## Conclusion

The case demonstrates the importance of transparency, informed consent and responsible data sharing. Organizations handling personal data should implement strong privacy controls and ensure that users understand how their information is collected and used.
